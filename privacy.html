Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

The Hammer List Inc. ("The Hammer List," "we," "us," or "our") operates thehammerlist.com and provides appointment-booking and marketing services to home service contractors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or communicating with us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact information — name, business name, email address, phone number, and mailing address submitted through our website forms, chat widget, scheduling links, or SMS conversations.
  • Business information — details about your company, services, and service area provided during consultations or onboarding.
  • Communications — records of calls, emails, and text messages exchanged with us.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically through standard analytics tools and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide our services;
  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments and consultations;
  • To send service-related communications, including by SMS where you have opted in;
  • To improve our website and services;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

SMS Terms & Consent

By providing your phone number and opting in through our website forms, chat widget, or by texting us, you consent to receive text messages from The Hammer List Inc. related to your inquiry, appointments, and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at the email below. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Our SMS program is governed by these terms together with our Terms and Conditions.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business (e.g., scheduling, payment processing, communications platforms), bound to use your information only to provide services to us;
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.

For clarity and consistent with Section 3: text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve our services. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email address below. To stop receiving text messages, reply STOP to any message. To unsubscribe from emails, use the link included in the email or contact us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
