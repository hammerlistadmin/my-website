FAQ

Common questions.

How much does it cost?
+
A flat monthly retainer plus your ad spend. No per-lead fees, no hidden charges, no "credits" that expire. You'll know your exact cost before we start — we break it all down on your strategy call. Nothing hits your card without you knowing why.
How fast do I see results?
+
Most partners see their first booked appointment within the first few days. Some within 48 hours. Not leads — actual appointments with confirmed homeowners on your calendar. We don't launch and wait. We launch and push.
What if I don't get 15 appointments?
+
You don't pay us. Simple as that. No runaround, no "credits" to a platform you'll never use again, no 30-day dispute process. We either deliver or you keep your money.
Do I share leads with other contractors?
+
Never. This isn't Angi — we don't sell your lead to 4-8 other guys and let you fight over it. You are the only contractor in your city for your trade. Every appointment is yours alone. Zero competition.
What makes you different from Angi, HomeAdvisor, or Thumbtack?
+
They sell leads. We book appointments. They share your lead with a half-dozen competitors. We give you exclusive territory. They lock you into annual contracts and charge you for wrong numbers. We work month to month and guarantee results. The FTC fined HomeAdvisor $7.2M for deceptive lead practices. We've never had a single complaint.
What do I need to get started?
+
A phone, a calendar, and a service area. We build the ads, the landing pages, and the follow-up system. You don't touch any of it. You just show up to appointments.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
+
No. Month to month. If we don't perform, you walk. No cancellation fees, no termination penalties, no fine print. We keep you because we deliver, not because we trapped you.
What if I've been burned by marketing agencies before?
+
Most of our partners have. That's why we don't sell promises — we guarantee appointments. 15 booked in 30 days or you don't pay. If an agency ever told you "leads take time" or "it's a branding play" — that was a red flag. Results show up in the first few days or something's wrong.
